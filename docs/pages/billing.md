# RIS-iPACX: Billing & GST Compliance

The Billing module handles financial transactions and invoicing for radiology services.

## 1. Invoicing Workflow
- **Auto-Generation**: Invoices are created automatically when a patient is registered for a study.
- **GST Compliance**: Support for SGST/CGST/IGST calculations as per Indian medical billing standards.

## 2. Payment Management
- **Payment Status**: Tracks `Pending`, `Partial`, and `Paid` states.
- **Methods**: Supports Cash, Card, UPI, and Corporate Billing (Insurance).

## 3. Financial Reporting
- **Daily Collections**: Breakdown of daily earnings by modality and payment method.
- **Tax Reports**: Exportable data for GST filing and auditing.
